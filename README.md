# Re-Examination-Management-System
Description: The Re-Examination Management System (REMS) is a web-based platform designed to simplify and digitize the re-examination process in educational institutions. Traditionally, students must complete manual paperwork, pay fees offline, and wait for administrative approval, often leading to errors, delays, and lack of transparency. REMS address these challenges by providing a centralized and user-friendly online interface for students, teachers, and administrators.

The system enables students to apply for re-examinations, make payments, and access their results from a unified dashboard. Teachers can verify applications, upload marks, and manage results, while administrators can oversee fee structures, issue notices, and monitor overall activity. Additional features such as online payment receipts, digital document uploads, previous-year question papers, and automated notifications enhance usability and efficiency.
